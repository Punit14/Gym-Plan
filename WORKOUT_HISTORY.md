# Workout Plan History

Log of changes made to the weekly plan in [index.html](index.html), newest first. Keep entries short: date, what changed, why.

## 2026-10-25 — Belly fat focus tweaks
- Monday: "Cardio Finisher" switched to HIIT intervals (30 sec sprint / 60 sec walk × 5) instead of steady incline walk — HIIT burns more fat in the same time.
- Monday: added Plank Hold at the end — Monday had no direct ab work before.
- Tuesday: added a cardio finisher + ab move — Tuesday was the only day in the week with zero cardio and zero ab work.
- Reason: goal is visible belly fat reduction within a month. Forearms intentionally left as regular (no extra focus) this round.

## 2026-10-25 — Chest exercise swap
- Monday: "Pec Deck Fly" replaced with "Cable Flyes" (same target: Chest). Cables keep tension through the full range, unlike the pec deck machine.

## 2026-10-02 (approx) — Leg Day moved to Friday
- Swapped "Leg Day" (was Wednesday) with "Compound Day (Extended)" (was Friday).
- Wednesday's Compound Day later had its "Seated Shoulder Press" replaced with "Tricep Pushdown" — shoulders were getting hit Wednesday (compound day) and Thursday (dedicated shoulder day) back-to-back with no rest in between.

## 2026-09-29 (approx) — Week restructured to start Monday
- Previously Monday was "Rest & Recovery" and Sunday was "Active Recovery (Extended)".
- Rotated the whole week back one day so training starts Monday (Push Day) and Sunday becomes the single full rest day.
- Resulting order: Mon Push → Tue Pull → Wed Compound (Extended) → Thu Shoulder → Fri Leg → Sat Active Recovery (Extended) → Sun Rest & Recovery.

## Goals tracked
- Belly fat reduction (cardio + ab volume spread across the week).
- Arm/forearm size (secondary, not currently a focus round).
- Original plan also targets general strength/chest-triceps/back-biceps/leg/shoulder hypertrophy within a 1-hour session cap.
